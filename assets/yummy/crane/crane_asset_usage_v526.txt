[v529 additional notes]
- descend_core.png was replaced with the newly supplied artwork.
- Root cause of the one-frame break after arm closure: the split descend and success/fail
  layers use different source canvas sizes; swapping the core/arm image sources at the same
  moment as the state-class reset could expose a transient mixed frame.
- success.png and fail.png were promoted from useless/ and are now used as preloaded
  whole-frame artwork for the upward success/fail states. Because they are already decoded
  and switch by class opacity only, the closure -> lift boundary does not mix layers.
- The separated descend layers remain in use for the arm-closing animation.
